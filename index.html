PivotView

PivotView is an Excel-style pivot table for Power BI: drag-and-drop field arrangement, hierarchies with subtotals, filtering, sorting, Top N, conditional formatting, saved views, and rich copy-to-Excel — all rendered natively in your report. PivotView makes no external network calls and collects no telemetry; your data never leaves Power BI.
