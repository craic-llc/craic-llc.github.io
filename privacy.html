Privacy Policy — PivotView

CRAiC LLC (Cost Readiness Analytics) · Effective date: August 14, 2026

This policy covers the PivotView custom visual for Microsoft Power BI, published by CRAiC LLC. The short version: PivotView does not collect, transmit, or store your data anywhere outside your Power BI report.

What data PivotView processes

PivotView renders the data you place into it inside Power BI. All processing happens locally, inside the Power BI sandbox on your device or in your organization's Power BI service session. Your data never leaves Power BI, and it is never sent to CRAiC LLC or to any third party by the visual.

What PivotView stores

PivotView saves its configuration — field layout, filters, sort order, saved views, themes, and display options — inside your Power BI report file, using Power BI's standard settings storage. This configuration stays with your report, under your control, wherever the report lives.

No telemetry, no tracking, no external calls

  • PivotView contains no telemetry or analytics of any kind.
  • It makes no network requests to CRAiC LLC or any external service.
  • It sets no cookies and uses no tracking technologies.

Licensing

License keys (issued directly by CRAiC LLC for paid tiers) are validated locally inside the visual using a cryptographic signature. A key contains only the licensee name and license dates that you provide to us at the time of purchase. Entering a key does not transmit anything anywhere — validation is fully offline.

Where Microsoft's built-in Power BI licensing service applies, PivotView asks that service whether a license is active; that check happens entirely between Power BI and Microsoft, and no information is sent to CRAiC LLC.

Information we receive outside the visual

If you contact us for support or purchase a license directly, we receive the information you choose to share (such as your name, email address, and organization). We use it only to provide support and fulfill your license, and we do not sell or share it.

Children's privacy

PivotView is a business analytics tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address with a new effective date. Because the visual makes no external calls, changes would only ever concern how we handle support and licensing interactions — never in-visual data collection.
